# Trinkets Abroad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Trinkets Abroad game ("the game") uses, who receives it and the choices you have. The game is published by Byte & Rise ("we", "us"). If you have a question, write to us at [contact@byteandrise.com](mailto:contact@byteandrise.com).

## The short version

- There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
- We do not run servers for the game. Your progress is saved only on your device.
- We do not use analytics tools of our own.
- The game shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google receives information from your device to show and measure ads. On iPhone and iPad, Google gets your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking.
- Purchases are paid through **Apple**. We use **RevenueCat** to check purchases and restore them. RevenueCat receives a record of your purchases and a random identifier, not your name or payment details.

## Information stored on your device

The game keeps a save file (`save.json`) in its private storage on your device. It holds your progress, coins, boosters, lives, passport stamps, settings and the answers you gave to the tracking, consent and notification questions. This file is not sent to us or to anyone else. Like other app data, it is included in the device backups you make (iCloud or a computer).

Reminder notifications (for example "your lives are full") are scheduled by iOS on your device. They do not come from a server, and the game does not use push notifications. You can turn them off in **Settings › Notifications › Trinkets Abroad**.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and paid for by ads: banners, ads between levels, and rewarded ads you choose to watch. The ads are served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects:

- **Device identifiers:** the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see below), and identifiers that are limited to apps from the same developer.
- **Advertising and interaction data:** which ads were shown, taps and video views, app launches.
- **Approximate location:** derived from your IP address. The game does not ask for your precise location.
- **Diagnostics:** performance data and crash logs from the ads SDK.

How Google uses this information is described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Tracking permission (iPhone and iPad).** After your first level, iOS asks whether the game may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose **Ask App Not to Track**, Google does not receive your advertising identifier and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this at any time in **Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking**.

**Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland.** In these regions the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is loaded. You can accept personalised ads, or refuse them; if you refuse, Google shows non-personalised or limited ads, which still use some information (such as approximate location and the ad interaction) to work and to prevent fraud. To change your choice later, tap **Privacy options** in the game.

**Removing ads.** The "Remove ads" purchase switches off banners and ads between levels. Rewarded ads stay available, and you only see them when you choose to watch one for a reward.

## Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

In-app purchases are paid through your Apple account. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Apple's handling of your data is described in [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm purchases, give you what you bought, restore purchases on a new device and prevent fraud. RevenueCat receives:

- the purchase record from the App Store: product, transaction identifier, date, price and currency, store country;
- a random identifier created by the game on your device, which is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID;
- basic technical information: app version, iOS version and device language.

We do not use this information to track you, and we cannot use it to identify you. See [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## What we do not do

- We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or any health data.
- We do not use analytics or crash reporting tools of our own. The Unity engine's analytics and crash reporting services are switched off.
- We do not sell your personal information. The sharing with Google for advertising is described above, and you can limit it with the choices in that section.

## Children

The game is rated 4+ on the App Store, which means its content is suitable for all ages. It is not directed at children: it is made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country), and we do not have accounts or a way to receive such information from players. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

## Keeping and deleting information

- **On your device:** deleting the game deletes its save file and its scheduled notifications. Copies can remain in device backups you made earlier.
- **At Google:** Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies. You can reset your device's advertising identifier, or keep tracking off, in iOS Settings.
- **At RevenueCat:** purchase records are kept while they are needed to restore purchases and for legal and tax reasons. Because the record is anonymous, we can only find it if you send us your App Store order ID (from your Apple purchase receipt email). We will then ask RevenueCat to delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, the UK or some US states), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, to object to or limit its use, to opt out of targeted advertising and to withdraw consent. You can use the in-game **Privacy options** and the iOS **Tracking** setting at any time, or write to us. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, the legal bases are: your consent (personalised ads and tracking), our legitimate interest in funding a free game with ads and in preventing fraud (non-personalised ads, fraud checks), and the performance of a contract (delivering and restoring purchases).

## International transfers

Google and RevenueCat are based in the United States and may process information there. They use legal safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

## Security

The game sends information only through the Apple, Google and RevenueCat software in it, over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We hold no player data ourselves.

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version at this address and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the App Store release notes.
