# Trinkets Abroad – Support

Need help with Trinkets Abroad? Most answers are below. If yours is not, write to us at [contact@byteandrise.com](mailto:contact@byteandrise.com) and we will reply as soon as we can.

## How do I play?

Turn and zoom the floating bazaar to find trinkets, then tap one to drop it into your tray. Three of the same item clear from the tray. Clear every goal before the timer runs out. If the tray fills up with no match, the level is lost and you try again.

Stuck? Use a booster:
- **Magnet** pulls a matching set into the tray.
- **Undo** puts the last item back.
- **Shuffle** mixes the items in the bazaar.
- **Freeze** stops the timer for a while.

## I lost my progress. Can you restore it?

Your progress is saved only on your device; the game has no account or cloud save. If you moved to a new device, restore it from an iCloud or computer backup of the old device, and your progress comes with it. If you deleted the game without a backup, we have no copy and cannot bring it back.

## How do I get back my "Remove ads" purchase on a new device?

Open the shop and tap **Restore purchases**, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. Coins, packs and other consumable items cannot be restored.

## I bought "Remove ads" but I still see an ad.

"Remove ads" switches off banners and the ads between levels. Rewarded ads, which give you coins in the shop or extra seconds after a lost level, stay available, but you only see them when you choose to watch one. If a banner or an ad between levels still appears, tap **Restore purchases** in the shop, then restart the game. If that does not help, write to us with the App Store order ID from your Apple receipt email.

## A purchase did not arrive.

Check your internet connection, then close and reopen the game. If the item is still missing after a few minutes, write to us with the App Store order ID from your Apple receipt email.

## How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in with your Apple ID and choose the purchase.

## How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

- **Tracking:** iOS **Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking**, then turn Trinkets Abroad on or off.
- **Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** tap **Privacy options** in the game to see Google's consent form again.

More in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

## How do I turn notifications on or off?

iOS **Settings › Notifications › Trinkets Abroad**. The game only sends reminders from your device, such as when your lives are full or a chest is ready, at most twice a day and not at night.

## Can I play offline?

Yes, the levels work without an internet connection. Ads and purchases need one.

## The game crashed or something looks wrong.

Write to us at [contact@byteandrise.com](mailto:contact@byteandrise.com) with:
- your device model and iOS version (Settings › General › About),
- the game version (bottom of the App Store page, or Settings › General › iPhone Storage › Trinkets Abroad),
- what you were doing when it happened, and a screenshot if you can.
